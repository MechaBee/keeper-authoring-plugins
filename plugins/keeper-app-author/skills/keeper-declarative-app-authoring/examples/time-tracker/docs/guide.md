# Time Tracker

Time Tracker is where your team records the hours it works, so the people who run projects can see
where time went and bill it. You log your own time; administrators maintain the projects you log
against and read the team-wide reports.

## How a week works

1. Pick the project you worked on, say how long, and save. That is a **time entry**.
2. Entries land in the **week** that contains their date, and in the **accounting period** — the
   calendar month — that contains it.
3. At the end of a month an administrator **locks** the period. Locked months cannot be changed
   any more; everything before that is yours to correct at any time.

There is no submit-and-approve step. Your entries count as soon as you save them, which is why the
month lock matters: it is the deadline.

## The words the app uses

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Client** | Who the work is for. Every project belongs to one. |
| **Project** | What you log time against. Projects are **open** or **closed**; closed ones take no new time. |
| **Task** | An optional breakdown inside a project. You can always leave it empty. |
| **Period** | A calendar month, **open** or **locked**. Locking freezes that month. |
| **Week** | Monday to Sunday. The grid in *My week* is one of these. |
| **Billable** | Whether this time can be charged to the client. Each project has a default; you can override it per entry. |

## Where to go

- **My entries** — add time and correct it. This is where most days start and end.
- **My week** — a Monday-to-Sunday grid of your hours, with weekly and daily totals.
- **Projects** — see what you have access to, and star the ones you use daily.
- **My reports** — filter and export your own time, including when you are an administrator.
- **Team reports**, **Setup**, **Access control** — administrators only.
