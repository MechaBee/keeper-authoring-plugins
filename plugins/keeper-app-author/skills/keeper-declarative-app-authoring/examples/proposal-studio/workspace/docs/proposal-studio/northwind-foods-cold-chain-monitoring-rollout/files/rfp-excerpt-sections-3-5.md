# Northwind Foods RFP NF-2026-14: sections 3 to 5

## 3. Scope

3.1 Temperature monitoring for 42 refrigerated vehicles operating from Bristol, Avonmouth and
Swindon depots.

3.2 The supplier shall provide alerting outside office hours. Northwind's transport office is
staffed 05:00 to 20:00 only.

## 4. Requirements

4.1 Alerts within 5 minutes of a temperature excursion of 2 °C or more above setpoint.

4.2 Compatibility with the existing fleet of Thermo King SR-4 and SR-5 units. Six older trailers
use Carrier Transicold Vector 1550 units.

4.3 Audit exports acceptable to our retail customers, currently Tesco and Waitrose.

4.4 Data hosted in the UK.

## 5. Timeline

5.1 The Bristol depot (18 vehicles) shall be live before 15 December 2026.

5.2 The remaining depots shall follow in Q1 2027.
