# Pricing principles

These principles apply to every proposal. Prices themselves come from the price book and need the
sales director's approval; never put a figure in a proposal that has not been approved.

- Cold Chain and Dispatch are priced per vehicle per month, on 36-month terms.
- Hardware is either bought outright or included in the monthly price.
- The monitoring service is priced per vehicle per month on top of Cold Chain.
- Implementation is a fixed fee per site, agreed before the proposal goes out.
- Pilots run for 90 days on one site at a reduced fixed fee, credited against the first year if the
  customer goes ahead.
