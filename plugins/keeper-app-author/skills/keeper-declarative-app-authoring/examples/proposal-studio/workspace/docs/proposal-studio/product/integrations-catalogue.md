# Integrations catalogue

## Refrigeration units (Cold Chain)

- Thermo King: SR-4 and later controllers, over the data port.
- Carrier Transicold: Vector and Supra series with TRU-Link.

## Telematics (both products)

- Webfleet, Samsara and Geotab: vehicle position and ignition, through their APIs.

## Warehouse and order systems (Dispatch)

- Manhattan, Blue Yonder and Microsoft Dynamics 365 SCM through their APIs.
- Any system that can drop a CSV order file on SFTP.

## Exports

- Audit reports as PDF and CSV.
- Retailer-specific temperature export formats: Tesco, Sainsbury's and Waitrose are available
  today. Others take four to six weeks to add.
