# Proposal template

Use these sections in this order. Leave out a section only when it truly does not apply.

## Why this matters to <customer>
Their problem in their own words, and what it costs them.

## What we propose
The products and services, mapped to their requirements.

## How we will roll it out
Sites, sequence and timeline, from the implementation playbook.

## Why Larkspur
Two or three proof points: references, certifications, a case study.

## Commercials
Pricing structure only, unless approved figures are provided.

## Assumptions and open questions
Everything we assumed and everything we still need to confirm.

## Next steps
Who does what next, with dates where agreed.
