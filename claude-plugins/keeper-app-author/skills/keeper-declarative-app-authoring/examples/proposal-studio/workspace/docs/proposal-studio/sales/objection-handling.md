# Objection handling

**"We already have telematics."**
Good, we use it. Cold Chain adds what telematics does not see: the refrigeration unit itself. We
read the controller, not just a probe in the cab.

**"Nobody would watch the alerts at night."**
That is what the monitoring service is for. Our desk watches every alert and phones your duty
contact.

**"Our drivers won't use another app."**
Dispatch replaces the paper run sheet and the proof-of-delivery device; it is one app, not an extra
one.

**"Rollout will disrupt operations."**
We fit around your schedule, one site at a time, and the next site starts only after you sign off.
