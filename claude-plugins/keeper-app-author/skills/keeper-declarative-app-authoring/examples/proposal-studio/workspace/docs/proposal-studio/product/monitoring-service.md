# Monitoring service overview (2025)

The monitoring service is our 24/7 desk in Bristol that watches Cold Chain alerts on the customer's
behalf. It is sold per vehicle per month, on top of Cold Chain.

## What the desk does

1. Receives every alert the customer's rules raise.
2. Checks it against live data (is the door open, is the unit defrosting).
3. Calls the customer's duty contact, following their escalation order, until someone accepts it.
4. Logs the call and outcome against the excursion, so it appears in the audit trail.

## Service levels in the 2025 contract

- The desk starts working an alert within 5 minutes, at any hour.
- The first call to the customer is made within 15 minutes of the alert.

These figures are from the 2025 standard contract. Check with the service manager before quoting
different ones.
