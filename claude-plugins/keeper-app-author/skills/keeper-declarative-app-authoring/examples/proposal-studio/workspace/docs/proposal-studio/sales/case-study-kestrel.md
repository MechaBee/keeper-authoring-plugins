# Case study: Kestrel Pharma

Kestrel Pharma distributes temperature-sensitive medicines from two warehouses to 900 pharmacies.

## The problem

Their quality team spent two days each month assembling temperature evidence for GDP audits from
data loggers, spreadsheets and driver notes.

## What we did

- Fitted Cold Chain to 18 vans and the two warehouse cold rooms in seven weeks.
- Set alert rules per product range, with the monitoring service covering nights and weekends.
- Built audit reports matching their GDP procedure.

## Results after one year

- Audit preparation went from two days a month to under an hour.
- Two excursions caught overnight by the monitoring desk; both loads were saved.
- An MHRA inspection in 2025 raised no findings on temperature control.
