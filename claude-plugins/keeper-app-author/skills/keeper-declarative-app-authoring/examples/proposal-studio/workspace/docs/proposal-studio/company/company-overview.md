# Larkspur Systems: company overview

Larkspur Systems builds software and hardware for companies that move goods which must arrive on
time and in the right condition: food distributors, pharmaceutical wholesalers, logistics providers
and healthcare transport services.

## At a glance

- Founded in 2014 in Bristol, UK. Offices in Bristol and Rotterdam.
- 140 employees, 45 of them in engineering and 20 in the monitoring and support desk.
- 310 customers across the UK, Ireland, the Netherlands and Belgium.
- Two products: **Larkspur Cold Chain** (temperature monitoring for vehicles and stores) and
  **Larkspur Dispatch** (route planning and dispatch).

## How we work with customers

Every customer gets a named implementation lead for the rollout and a named account manager after
go-live. We roll out site by site, starting with the site that has the most to gain, and we do not
start the next site until the customer signs off the previous one.

## What we are not

We do not sell vehicle hardware other than our own gateways and sensors, and we do not resell
fleet telematics. We integrate with the telematics and warehouse systems customers already have.
