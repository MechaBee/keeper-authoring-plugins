# Implementation playbook

Our standard rollout takes six weeks for the first site and two to three weeks for each site after
that.

## Weeks 1 to 2: design

- Kick-off with the customer's project lead, operations and quality.
- Agree alert rules, escalation contacts and report formats.
- Survey the first site's vehicles and units.

## Weeks 3 to 4: fit and configure

- Fit gateways (about 15 vehicles a day with two engineers).
- Connect telematics and order systems.
- Train the control room and quality team.

## Weeks 5 to 6: run and sign off

- Two weeks of live running with daily check-ins.
- Sign-off meeting. The next site starts only after sign-off.

## What we need from the customer

A project lead with a few hours a week, vehicle access for fitting, and the contact list for
escalations.
