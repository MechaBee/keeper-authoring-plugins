# Security and compliance summary

## Certifications

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**, certified since 2019, last surveillance audit March 2026.
- **Cyber Essentials Plus**, renewed annually.
- We do not hold SOC 2. Customers who ask for it receive our ISO 27001 statement of applicability
  and the latest penetration test summary under NDA.

## Data

- Customer data is hosted in AWS eu-west-2 (London) with backups in eu-west-1 (Ireland).
- Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2 or later).
- Temperature records are kept for 7 years by default, which covers GDP and retailer audit
  requirements. Customers can ask for a shorter period.

## Access

- Single sign-on through Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace or any SAML 2.0 provider.
- Role-based access per site. Audit logs of every user action are kept for 2 years.

## Regulated customers

Our Cold Chain audit trail supports EU Good Distribution Practice (GDP) Chapter 9 and the
temperature-record requirements in BRCGS Storage and Distribution. We have supported 14 customer
audits by the MHRA and retailers since 2022 with no findings against our system.
